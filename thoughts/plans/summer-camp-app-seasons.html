Plan: Summer Camp App Seasons — start Summer 2027, carry forward select camps

Goal: In the Summer Camp App, Christie can start a new summer the way she starts a semester in the Classbook — create "Summer 2027" with its own dates and week structure, carry forward the camps she chooses from 2026 (now, or one at a time later), plan 2027 while 2026 stays intact as history, and publish 2027 camps to the Classbook when ready.
App: summer-camp-app (Summer Camp 2026) — js/app.js, js/config.js, index.html. Consumer: tinker-spring-curriculum (The Classbook) via classbook-camp-seasons.html.
Context: Created Sep 20, 2026. Christie: "I build all backend summer info in [the Summer Camp App], and then publish it to classbook … I would need to be able to set a new summer in the Summer Camp app, similar to how I do in the classbook with new semesters. But I'd want to be able to copy select Summer Camps into the next year, and/or toggle them into the upcoming year at a later date too." This plan therefore owns the season — its creation, the shared-collection backfill, and carry-forward. The Classbook plan becomes the consumer side and depends on this one.
Risk: MEDIUM-HIGH — 17 collections become season-aware; a backfill of every existing doc; carry-forward copies documents between seasons (the classic place for duplicates or a copy that silently overwrites). Every data-write phase gets the full data-safety process (red test on real Firestore, dual implementation review, Console spot-check).
execution-ready: false — Scoping complete: D1–D7 answered by Christie Sep 20, 2026 (see each decision box and the Decisions Log). Next: detailed Phase 1 design → second-model plan review.

Context — what the app is today

  • Single-season by construction. js/config.js APP_CONFIG: appName: 'Summer Camp 2026', startDate: '2026-05-26', endDate: '2026-08-11', numWeeks: 11, and a hard-coded weekStructure (days off, notes, date ranges per week) with 17 consumers in app.js. Date pickers in index.html are bounded to 2026.
  • Camps come from Google Sheets. APP_CONFIG.sheetGIDs (campTopics, campSessions, masterCurriculum, projectDetails, materialsByTopic, needToOrder, prepHelpQueue, teacherHelpQueue) feed imports into Firestore; camp docs are then edited in-app (saveCampOverride() etc.). A "new summer" therefore means: a 2027 sheet (or a season-tagged import) plus in-app editing — not a blank "create camp" form today (D2).
  • 17 summerCamps_* collections, all with Firestore rules already: curriculum, schedule, lessonData (shared with the Classbook), projectDetails, materialsHub, needToOrder, stockItems, prepHelpQueue, weeklyPrep, campComplete, openStudio, kidNotes, notesNextYear, daysOff, team, projectLibrary, settings. None carries a season/year field (verified on real docs Sep 20: curriculum 48 docs, schedule 229 docs — no year anywhere) — except Kid Notes and Notes-for-Next-Year, which already have a 2025/2026/All year toggle in the UI (index.html:603-606), so a per-year concept exists in two places and can be generalized rather than invented.
  • Publish to Classbook sets publishedToClassbook: true (+ publishedToClassbookAt) on camp/project docs (app.js:~12065-12141); the Classbook reads every camp doc and shows the published ones. Without a season, a published 2027 camp would appear inside the Classbook's Summer 2026.
  • What "copy a camp" has to carry: the camp's curriculum doc (campTopic, teacher, blocks with Monday–Friday project titles, age range, bins, notes, preset projects), its project details (per project), its materials (materialsHub entries keyed campTopic|||project). What it must not carry: schedule/sessions and enrollment (2027 has its own schedule), campComplete flags, kid notes, prep-help queue items, needToOrder — and, by default, teachers' 2026 lesson plans (D3b).

AMENDED Sep 20, 2026 (Christie's follow-up: don't lose the rename-reassign safety feature; suggested a "confirm delete" pop-up or a Cut Bank to recover from). Agreed — "default delete" above was too aggressive; the reassign step is exactly the right safety net while a curriculum is being reshaped. Revised design, which also mirrors a pattern Christie already knows from the Classbook's Cut Bank: when a project disappears from a 2027 camp's grid (rename or removal), the existing orphan modal appears listing every affected item (project details, materials, draft plans) with three choices per item — Reassign to another project in the camp (pre-selected when a rename is obvious), Move to Cut Bank (the default for a removed project — fully recoverable), or Delete (behind a confirm). The Cut Bank is a soft delete, not a new collection: the project's docs stay where they are with status: 'cut', cutAt, cutFromCamp, are hidden from every grid/dashboard, and are listed in a per-season Cut Bank panel from which "Restore to camp/block" puts them back (re-keying to the destination, the same way the Classbook's paste-from-Cut-Bank works). Permanent delete happens only from the Cut Bank, with a confirm. Nothing is destroyed by reshaping a curriculum; the Classbook still shows exactly the live project flow because cut items are excluded from what it reads. No new collection → no rules change. Added to Phase 3 acceptance.
D4 — RESOLVED Sep 20, 2026 (Christie: yes, 2026 should become read-only, "with the caveat that I can still copy data over into 2027, delete things from that copy and change things from it — that way if I'm working in a camp that has been carried forward into '27 and I'm accidentally on '26, I don't change 2026 records by mistake").
