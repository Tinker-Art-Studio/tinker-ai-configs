D-5 — The stack, and the shared Denver date + status module execution-ready: false

Acceptance criteria

  • CLAUDE.md records the stack (vanilla JS, Firebase v12 modular, PWA manifest, Netlify) and the three locations (Firestore nam5 / trigger nam5 / functions us-central1). DATA-MODEL.md records C6 (as an invariant), C7 and C8; SPEC §14's example is corrected.

  • Dates: YYYY-MM-DD strings are parsed by taking the fields apart, never through new Date(string) (which reads a bare date as UTC midnight — a day early in Denver; the bug Phase B fixed in Membership Manager). Strict format: impossible dates (2026-02-30), a leap day, and wrong shapes are "malformed".
  • Weeks (C6, C7): ISO week keys with the ISO week-year — 2026-12-28 (Mon) → 2026-W53; 2027-01-01 (Fri) → 2026-W53; 2027-01-04 (Mon) → 2027-W01; single-digit weeks padded. A Sunday 10 PM–12:00 AM booking is 120 minutes in that Sunday's week and none in Monday's; a booking with any minute in the next day is reported malformed (no weekKey), never quietly given the start week. A week containing a DST change is still Mon–Sun (the March 2026 Denver week is 167 hours, November's 169) — the test that catches millisecond arithmetic.
  • DST: "now in Denver" and slot times are right across spring-forward (the missing 2 AM hour) and fall-back (the repeated 1 AM hour).
  • deriveStatus(source, todayDenver) — today is passed in, never read from the clock inside. It returns exactly the statuses in DATA-MODEL.md (active, paused, review, offboarded, not_yet, removed). Tests:
    • one case per rule 1–10;
    • order, by conflicts — for each rule, a case that also matches a later rule, asserting the earlier one wins: tombstone over everything; onboarding over a malformed last day; unknown stage over a current pause; a malformed last day over cancelled; a past last day over a current pause; cancelled with no last day over a malformed pause; offboarding with no last day over a current pause; a malformed pause entry over a different, valid current pause;
    • boundaries: a pause's last day is paused and the next day active (#35); the last access day has access and the next day is offboarded;
