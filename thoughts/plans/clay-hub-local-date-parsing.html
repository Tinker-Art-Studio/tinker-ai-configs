Clay Hub Membership — read dates as local calendar days

Repo: clay-hub-membership · Sep 25 2026 · follows clay-hub-pause-and-cancel-status-fixes (shipped 8ed73e2)

Goal

Every date in Membership Manager means the calendar day it says: "PAUSE TODAY" appears on the pause's start date (not the day before), pauses resume the day after they end, off-boarding members become cancelled the day after final access, and the Email Alexis message states the real final access date.

Research findings

  • Stored dates are mostly YYYY-MM-DD (from date inputs). new Date('2026-09-26') is UTC midnight = Sep 25 evening in Colorado; after setHours(0) it becomes Sep 25. ~30 comparison sites do this.
  • formatDate already parses YYYY-MM-DD as local — so cards display correctly while alerts/transitions fire a day early.
  • 7 writes use date.toISOString().split('T')[0] (UTC date). Only stayOnListDate = new Date().toISOString()… (move to Deferred) actually lands on the wrong day (after ~6pm → tomorrow); the others operate on local-midnight dates and happen to be right in US time, but are fragile.
  • emailAlexis / getNextBusinessDay comment claims to "match UTC-midnight parsing used by formatDate" — wrong since formatDate went local. Result: the email says final access is a day early, and the "on or shortly after" task date equals the final access date instead of the next business day.
  • Non-YYYY-MM-DD strings ("Oct 30, 2026", "10/29/2026", ISO timestamps) already parse as local/instant and must keep doing so.

Phase 1 — Shared helpers + tests execution-ready: false

In js/member-status.js: parseLocalDate(value) — exact YYYY-MM-DD → local midnight; Date → copy; anything else → new Date(value) unchanged. toLocalYMD(date) — local YYYY-MM-DD. parsePauseDay switches to parseLocalDate. Tests pin TZ=America/Denver inside the test file so they fail against UTC parsing on any machine.

Acceptance: tests for boundaries (start today, end today, day after end) pass in Denver; non-date-only strings parse exactly as before.

Phase 2 — Switch every site in app.js (single commit, single push)

  • Replace new Date(storedField) with parseLocalDate(storedField) at all stored-date parse sites (pause/cancel alerts, pending count, View list, checkExpiredOffboarding, stale scheduledPause cleanup, completeWorkflow, pause eligibility ×2, commitment cycle, quick-log process date, Email Alexis, Sawyer termEnd, custom alerts).
  • Replace toISOString().split('T')[0] with toLocalYMD(...) at all 7 write sites.
  • Leave new Date(), timestamps (invitationSent, form timestamp), and sorting untouched.
  • All in one push: partial conversion re-creates the flip-flop bug the last review caught.
Acceptance: grep shows no new Date( on a stored date field and no toISOString().split('T')[0]; tests green; harness shows a pause starting tomorrow is "upcoming", not "TODAY".

BDD scenarios

Happy: Given a scheduled pause starting 2026-09-26 and today Sep 25 (Denver), when the page loads, then the banner says "1 pause upcoming this week", not "to process TODAY". On Sep 26 it says TODAY.

Resume boundary: Given a pause ending 2026-09-25, on Sep 25 the member stays Paused; on Sep 26 they auto-resume.

Off-boarding boundary: Given final access 2026-11-29, the member is Off-Boarding through Nov 29 and Cancelled on Nov 30.

Email Alexis: Given final access 2026-11-29 (a Sunday), the email says "Sunday, November 29, 2026" and the task date "Monday, November 30, 2026".

Evening write: Given it is 9pm Sep 25 in Denver, moving someone to Deferred records 2026-09-25.

Non-ISO strings: "Oct 30, 2026" and ISO timestamps parse exactly as before.

Failure path: Unparseable dates still yield Invalid Date and hit the same isNaN guards as today.

Firebase safety checklist

  • No rules change, no new collection, no bulk import/delete, no deploy of Firebase.
  • No stored data is migrated. Existing YYYY-MM-DD values keep their text; they are simply read as the day they say. Writes use the same field names and format.
  • One-time effect on first load after deploy: members whose transition was pulled a day early won't be affected (already transitioned); members on a boundary day will transition one day later than they would have. Expected and correct.
  • Write path unchanged (full-doc set() remains flagged separately).

Out of scope

  • Commitment-cycle numbers looking wrong (e.g. Anne Pizzi) — parked by Christie; parsing fix may shift it by a day but won't fix the underlying logic.
  • Future-dated pause not auto-flipping to Paused; full-doc rewrites on load.

Completeness / interruption
