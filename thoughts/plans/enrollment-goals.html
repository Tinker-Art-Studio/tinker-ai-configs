Goal

Christie opens a Goals tab on the Enrollment Board and sees two charts, one for Tinker Art Studio and one for Clay Hub. Each shows the year's progress in enrollment units against fixed Good / Better / Best targets, with a bar per program underneath. She screenshots or downloads it straight into the staff slide deck. Enrollment goals drive staff bonuses, so every number has to be explainable and consistent.

The unit

1 unit = one Youth Tinker semester seat = $406 tuition. Every seat, party, member-month or camp week is worth its tuition ÷ $406. Materials are left out because they're a wash. Prices are the current ones from the pricing calculator (tinker-pricing.netlify.app, read 2026-09-28). Every year is valued at the same price table, so year-over-year growth measures enrollment, not price increases.

Decisions

D1 — Two businesses, two goals (Christie, 2026-09-28)

Tinker and Clay Hub each get their own chart, own total and own Good / Better / Best targets, because Christie is splitting them into separate businesses. Sections are assigned by location: Clay Hub = CH, and everything else is Tinker, including off-site locations (Coal Creek, BMoCA). Private events at an off-site location are tracked under Tinker.

D2 — 2026 targets (Christie agreed, 2026-09-28)

The 2026 goals are being set with 3 months left, so they mostly describe a year that's already booked. Per business: Good = match 2025, and Better / Best = reachable Q4 wins (about +2% / +4% over 2025). The exact unit targets are computed in Phase 1 and confirmed by Christie before they're shown to staff. Real, forward-looking 2027 targets get set in January.

Resolved 2026-09-28: 2025 totals are split by business using 2026's Tinker / Clay Hub share for each program (Christie).

D3 — Members count in Clay Hub's goal (Christie, 2026-09-28)

0.64 units per member-month, 40 spots. They stay in Clay Hub's total. The chart labels them "at capacity" so it's clear they're a steady base, not a lever. v1 uses a set member count per month: 2026 = 40 every month; 2025 = opened Feb with ~20 founding members, rising steadily to 40 by November, full in December ≈ 340 member-months ≈ 218 units (Christie). Reading live counts from clayHub_members is a later option.

D4 — Tracked, not in the goal

Tinker Time, LAC and private events appear under a dashed line, as on the 2025 slide. Private events come from the private events app's Firestore data (privateEvents, stage confirmed or completed, grouped by event date and location). Board users are managers, and managers can already read that collection, so no rules change is needed.

D5 — Birthday parties come from the Sawyer calendar

Booked = party cards on the Scheduled month view. Offered = booked + cards on the Available month view (/portal/monthly_calendar/available), which lists unbooked slots. This was verified on 2026-09-28: Jan–Sep booked Tinker parties (39) exactly match the tip log. This adds a second page to the refresh's hard scope limit — approved by Christie 2026-09-28. It's still a calendar view with no names. Each refresh stores monthly counts: PARTIES: {"2026-10": {T: {booked, open}, C: {...}}}.

D6 — Where each piece lives

  • Price table and mapping rules go in template.html. They're public prices, and changing them is a code change with a test.
  • Targets, 2025 baseline and member counts go in a new doc, enrollmentBoard/goals. It's in the existing collection, so the existing rule covers it and no rules change is needed. Business targets stay behind sign-in rather than in the static build. Christie changes them by asking a session for a targeted edit, the same way as CANCELLED.
  • Party counts go in enrollmentBoard/current, written by the refresh.

D7 — Mapping is visible, never silent

Every section is mapped to a price row by rules (location, kind, name, time of day). The Goals tab has a "How units are counted" table listing each section with its units. A section no rule matches is listed under Not priced yet, not guessed. That matters because bonuses depend on these numbers.
