### Findings

1. **MED — `js/app.js:9778`, `css/styles.css:1995-2001`. Lesson Plans is the only tab where the badge isn't grouped.** `.camp-card-header` is `display:flex; justify-content:space-between` with no `flex-wrap`; inserting the badge as a third child strands it in the middle of the row instead of next to the status badge (Curriculum/Materials/Project Details all nest theirs in a gap'd flex div). Combined with `white-space:nowrap` on a ~31-character badge, a narrow viewport squeezes the camp name or overflows the card. *Fix:* wrap the badge + `statusBadge` in `<div style="display:flex;gap:0.5rem;align-items:center;flex-wrap:wrap;">`, as the other three tabs do.

2. **MED — `js/app.js:271-275`, effect at `js/app.js:329`.** Previously the `users` read rejected and `window.STAFF_NAMES` was never assigned; now it's set to `{}`. 3. **LOW — `js/app.js:524` + `536`, with `251`.** Click "Mark reviewed", then click Save while the write is in flight: `fields` skips the already-disabled review button; `setCampReviewed`'s `finally` then re-renders a *new* button carrying `disabled` (because `curriculumManualSaving`), which Save's `finally` can't re-enable.
