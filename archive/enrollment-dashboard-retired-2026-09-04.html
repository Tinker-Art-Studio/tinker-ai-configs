Getting a clean printout

This page runs inside a viewer frame, and a browser won't let a framed page decide where its own page breaks fall — it just slices the frame at each sheet. Two settings do all the work:

1. Pick what goes on the sheet. Set What above to Semester classes and the weekly grid lands on exactly one landscape page. Set it to Workshops & one-offs for a second sheet of the dated events. Leaving it on Everything runs the two together and the workshop list starts wherever the grid happens to end.

2. In the print dialog, set Pages to 1 (or 1-2 for Everything). The frame is taller than the schedule, so the browser offers several blank sheets after it — that space belongs to the viewer, not to this page, and limiting the range is the way past it.

Optional: under More settings, untick Headers and footers to drop the date and the claude.ai link — worth doing for anything a family sees.
